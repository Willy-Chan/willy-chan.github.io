**TL;DR:** I'm trying to write more. That's what this blog is for, and on it you're going to find a mix of personal essays as well as technical things that I just happen to be interested in. Hope it helps at least someone out there!

Last year, I made a resolution to document my life more deliberately: take more photos of important moments, jot down stray thoughts in a physical notebook, keep note of life goals and new hobbies, etc. But work, as it tends to do, got in the way and I was so caught up in meeting new people and experiencing new things that writing unfortunately had to be set on the backburner. That resolution has been gathering dust for a while now, but no longer!

This blog is my attempt to pick it back up. 

My plan is to essentially use this "blog" section of my website as basically a brain dump: on it you'll find my personal thoughts and essays about various topics as well as technical deep dives. I might add more organization to it in the future, but for now I think I just need a space on the internet to ruminate and whatnot.

Excited to see where this goes! And please reach out if you're interested in collaborating with me on anything.
