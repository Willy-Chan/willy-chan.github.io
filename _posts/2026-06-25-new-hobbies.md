title: Post-grad arc

---

**TL;DR:** I'm trying to write more. That's what this blog section is for, I've been finding a lot of joy in looking back at the past (mostly through photos) and I want to do something similar with my thoughts. 

Last year, I made a resolution to document my life more deliberately: take more photos of important moments, jot down stray thoughts in a physical notebook, keep note of life goals and new hobbies, etc. But work, as it tends to do, got in the way and I was so caught up in meeting new people and experiencing new things that writing unfortunately had to be set on the backburner. That resolution has been gathering dust for a while now, but no longer!

This blog is my attempt to pick it back up.

I've noticed something about the way I remember things. When I scroll through my camera roll, a photo isn't just a photo. I remember what I was doing, the story behind the frame, the lighting, the sounds, the music playing nearby. To anyone else it might look ordinary; to me it carries weight.

I do something similar with music. I keep playlists of everything I've listened to, in chronological order. When I go back and play an old song, I'm transported — not just to the melody, but to the exact state of mind I was in and the events unfolding around me at the time. It's a literal timeline of how I've felt over the years.

Documentation, nostalgia, the way history seems to rhyme with itself — all of it feels deeply meaningful to me. The last thing I want, decades from now, is to have lived interesting moments and let them fade.

Excited to see where this goes!
